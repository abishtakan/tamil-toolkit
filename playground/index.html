Tamil Toolkit Playground (தமிழ் கருவிப் பட்டறை)

Browser-based Tamil language workbench powered by Rust and WebAssembly. Processes text locally in your browser.

  • Bamini, TSCII & Legacy Tamil Font to Unicode Converter
  • Bharathi Tamil Braille Grade 1 Converter
  • Tamil99 Online Keyboard & Transliteration (ISO 15919, Tanglish)
  • Tamil Number to Words & Cheque Formatter (INR, LKR, SGD, USD)
  • Agara Varisai Tamil Alphabetical Sorter
  • Tamil Syllabic Hyphenator (Word, LibreOffice, InDesign)
  • Tamil Calendar & Panchangam (Thirukanitham & Vakya)

Open-source Rust, Python, and JavaScript/WebAssembly packages: github.com/abishtakan/tamil-toolkit.
