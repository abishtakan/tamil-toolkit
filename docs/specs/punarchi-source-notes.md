# Punarchi source digest: Nannūl Eḻuttatikāram

Status: working research aid. This is a page-indexed paraphrase, not a
translation, critical transcription, approved rule specification, or test data.
It helps reviewers find the relevant material in the selected edition. Do not
implement a rule from this digest alone.

## Edition and page handling

text and Sankara Namasivayar's Viruttiyurai, corrected by Sivagnana Munivar.

Institute of Tamil Studies publication 330, first edition, 600 pages. The
editorial preface says manuscript and print witnesses were compared and that
the root text, commentary, and Munivar's corrections are distinguished.

### Uyirīṟṟup Puṇariyal: 151–203

The opening material (151–157) defines the operation, distinguishes the two
major grammatical relation types, and explains unchanged versus changed joins.
The change discussion covers addition, alteration, and loss, their possible
positions, poetic variation, and the possibility of more than one change at a
boundary. The edition starts this section at [viewer page 218](https://www.tamilvu.org/slet/l0100/l0100pd1.jsp?auth_pub_id=17&bookid=6&pno=218)
with sūtra 151; sūtra 152 appears on [page 219](https://www.tamilvu.org/slet/l0100/l0100pd1.jsp?auth_pub_id=17&bookid=6&pno=219),
sūtra 153 on [page 221](https://www.tamilvu.org/slet/l0100/l0100pd1.jsp?auth_pub_id=17&bookid=6&pno=221),
sūtra 154 on [page 222](https://www.tamilvu.org/slet/l0100/l0100pd1.jsp?auth_pub_id=17&bookid=6&pno=222),
and sūtras 156–157 on [page 224](https://www.tamilvu.org/slet/l0100/l0100pd1.jsp?auth_pub_id=17&bookid=6&pno=224).

Sūtras 151 and 158 have explicit Tolkāppiyam citations in their commentaries:
viewer page 219 cites Tokai Marabu 2 while discussing natural/altered joining;
viewer page 226 cites Tokai Marabu 3. These are commentator-supplied
cross-references, not proof that the corresponding rules are equivalent.

Sūtras 158–164 move from general joining behavior into conditions involving
word classes, vowel-to-vowel contact, interrogative/demonstrative forms, and
short-u endings. Sūtra 158 begins on [viewer page 225](https://www.tamilvu.org/slet/l0100/l0100pd1.jsp?auth_pub_id=17&bookid=6&pno=225).
Its commentary on [page 226](https://www.tamilvu.org/slet/l0100/l0100pd1.jsp?auth_pub_id=17&bookid=6&pno=226)
explicitly cites Tolkāppiyam Tokai Marabu 3 as a comparison lead. That is a
commentator's cross-reference; it does not establish that the two rules are
identical.

Sūtras 165–187 organize special cases by vowel-final form and lexical class.
They include conditions for consonant strengthening, named word classes,
exceptions, and changes conditioned by the following word. Treat this as a
family of restricted rules, not one general doubling rule.

Sūtras 188–199 cover number-word joining and several changes to number forms.
Sūtras 200–203 close the section with special cases for particular vowel
endings and word classes. Exact conditions and outputs must be recorded
individually from the edition before implementation.

## Tolkāppiyam comparison record

Tolkāppiyam remains a separate comparative tradition. Its Eḻuttatikāram
contains candidate search areas in Puṇariyal, Tokai Marabu, Urubiyal, Uyir
Mayangiyal, Pulli Mayangiyal, and Kuṟṟiyal-ukarap Puṇariyal. For case-ending
comparisons that require the word chapter, a separate Collatikāram edition
must be pinned.

Two explicit cross-references are located so far: the Nannūl commentary on
sūtra 151 cites Tolkāppiyam Tokai Marabu 2 on TVA viewer page 219, and the
commentary on sūtra 158 cites Tokai Marabu 3 on page 226. These are
commentator-supplied primary-text locators, not claims of rule equivalence. The
[Project Madurai Tolkāppiyam mūlam](https://projectmadurai.org/pm_etexts/utf8/pmuni0100.html)
provides a discovery-only locator: its Eḻuttatikāram contents identify Tokai
Marabu as section 1.5, and the section includes numbered items 2 and 3. The
e-text does not identify its print base and has no commentary, so this confirms
only where to look, not the reading, interpretation, or equivalence in a
selected edition.

An earlier draft called the CICT-cataloged 1979 Eḻuttatikāram with
Iḷampūraṇar's commentary the selected comparison edition. That choice was
premature: it was made from the first catalog result without comparing later
editions or editorial method. No Tolkāppiyam comparison edition is now pinned.
The CICT PDF link for the 1979 Annamalai University edition, edited by Dr. Ku.
Sundaramoorthy, points to a private `192.168.0.169:5000` address, and its public
host path returned an error. Separately, Tamil Virtual Academy catalogs the
1979 volume with a flipbook reader and two PDF files. The TVA scan has now been
inspected. Its title verso identifies a second edition dated August 1979, and
the volume presents the root text, Iḷampūraṇar's commentary, and Ku.
Sundaramurthy's explanatory layer. Front matter (PDF pp. 11–12) discusses
earlier editions and editorial corrections; the inspected pages do not
establish a systematic manuscript-collation method. The scan is a usable
provisional witness, not a proven latest or definitive edition. TVA is the
usable access route.

encoding makes direct text extraction garbled; rendered pages remain legible.

TVA also catalogs the World Tamil Research Institute's 1982
[*Eḻuttatikāram: Puṇariyal Uraivaḷam*](https://tamildigitallibrary.in/Articles/%E0%AE%A8%E0%AF%82%E0%AE%B2%E0%AF%8D-27108-%E0%AE%A4%E0%AF%8A%E0%AE%B2%E0%AF%8D%E0%AE%95%E0%AE%BE%E0%AE%AA%E0%AF%8D%E0%AE%AA%E0%AE%BF%E0%AE%AF%E0%AE%B2%E0%AF%8D%20%E0%AE%8E%E0%AE%B4%E0%AF%81%E0%AE%A4%E0%AF%8D%E0%AE%A4%E0%AE%A4%E0%AE%BF%E0%AE%95%E0%AE%BE%E0%AE%B0%E0%AE%AE%E0%AF%8D-%E0%AE%AA%E0%AF%81%E0%AE%A3%E0%AE%B0%E0%AE%BF%E0%AE%AF%E0%AE%B2%E0%AF%8D),
series no. 45. The inspected scan identifies editor A. Sivalinganar, first
edition March 1982, and describes the work as a compilation of commentaries.
Its sample pages present distinct commentary sections. Use it as a
topic-specific interpretive cross-check, not as a standalone root-text edition.

CICT's [publications catalogue](https://publishingcatalogue.digitalarchives.cict.in/)
lists Prof. Palani Arangasamy's *Tolkāppiyam: Eḻuttatikāram; Iḷampūraṇar
Commentary* as a 2025 English research monograph, ISBN 978-93-49646-36-0,
xviii + 272 pages. CICT's [release announcement](https://cict.in/cict2023/)
records its release on 2026-06-05. It is a recent related publication, but its
English research-monograph classification does not make it a Tamil base-text
edition. Its source text and translation method remain to be checked.

A major textual-variation resource is the 1996 *Textual Variation of Tolkappiyam:
A Deep Study* (CICT's English catalog title), edited by K. M. Vēṅkaṭarāmaiyā, S.
V. Subramanian, and P. V. Nākarājan. CICT classifies it as a Text and hosts its
[full PDF](https://ctlt.cict.in/s_book/1tholkappiyam/1_tholkappiyam.pdf); the
[CiNii record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA40196488) gives the 1996 imprint and
extent xxxi + 424 pages. The preface and sample Puṇariyal pages were inspected.
The editors explain that they collated manuscript and printed witnesses and
present variant readings with discussion. They explicitly say they do not
attempt to establish one base reading. It is therefore a strong
variant-apparatus companion, not a definitive root-text edition.

The same-editor Annamalai edition has later catalog evidence: Thiagarajar
College cites a 1992 third edition, while CICT's [Semmozhi catalogue](https://library.cict.in/semmozhi/search_books.php?book=%E0%AE%87%E0%AE%B3%E0%AE%AE%E0%AF%8D%E0%AE%AA%E0%AF%82%E0%AE%B0%E0%AE%A3%E0%AE%B0%E0%AF%8D)
lists a matching title, editor, and publisher as 1996 and duplicate 1991
records. No later title verso or preface scan was located. Whether 1996 is a
revised edition, reprint, or cataloging discrepancy is unresolved. Syllabus
lists also identify Balasundaram's 2012 research commentary and Subramanian's
revised 2021 commentary; these are later interpretations, not proven critical
root-text editions.

**Decision for this milestone:** Nannūl remains the primary Punarchi source.
For Tolkāppiyam, retain the scanned 1979 Iḷampūraṇar edition only as a
provisional page-citation witness, and require the 1996 variant study as a
cross-check when a comparison is needed. The later 1992/1996 Sundaramurthy
copy is the next item to obtain and inspect before pinning a sole commentary
edition. The 2003 Tamilmann edition and 1982 WTRI compilation remain parallel
commentary witnesses. No rule equivalence or Tolkāppiyam-derived fixture is
approved. When a particular Nannūl rule requires comparison, record its exact
Tolkāppiyam source passage, edition and commentary layers, variants, and page
anchors rather than inferring equivalence from section headings or dates.

For topic discovery, the [GRETIL/TextGrid Nannūl TEI](https://textgridrep.org/browse/49trx.0)
records data entry from Project Madurai and has a CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license. Its
header leaves the underlying source field blank. It was used as a locator only;
no transliteration or examples from that TEI were copied into this repository.
