## 4. Field-Level Forensic Analysis & Provenance Separation

### 4.1 `primary_lemmas.csv` (44,229 rows, 17 columns)

- **Headword ordering (`word_id` 1..44,229):** Contiguous integers `1` through `44,229` with `398` adjacent identical headwords (POS/homonym splits). Only `4` first-character codepoint drops occur across the entire table — at `வௌவால்` (`43942`) -> `ழுஹர்` (`43943`) -> `றகரம்` (`43944`) -> `ஜக்கம்மா` (`43945`), and `ஹோரை` (`44216`) -> `க்ஷணம்` (`44217`) — plus minor Tamil collation order differences (such as placing Grantha/final letters after `வௌ` and ordering Tamil vowels `அ, ஆ...` relative to ஆய்தம் `ஃ`).
