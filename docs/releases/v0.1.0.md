## Compatibility and scope

- First public stable `0.1.0` release across crates.io (all 9 slim `Apache-2.0` crates, `< 10 MiB` each), PyPI (`tamil-toolkit 0.1.0` abi3 wheels + sdist), and npm (`tamil-toolkit@0.1.0` on `latest` dist-tag, Node + Browser WASM).
- Adds the `pub use tamil_translit as translit;` re-export on `tamil-toolkit` so all 8 underlying public crates are exposed on the Rust facade.
- Verified against `40,211` active golden vectors across Rust, Python, Node/Browser WASM, and Dart.
- **Bounded & experimental scope:** Legacy font codecs (11 fonts) and Bharathi Braille Grade 1 cover their declared bounded profiles. Four civil `tamil-calendar` suites (`calendar_year_cycle`, `calendar_official_holidays`, `calendar_tamil_display`, `calendar_thiruvalluvar_year`) are active; 13 Thirukanitham/Vakya ephemeris and observance suites remain experimental (`planned` pending independent witness review — see [numerals-calendar-candidate.md](numerals-calendar-candidate.md)). Planned NLP crates (Phases 9–17) and Dart/Pub or PostgreSQL distributions remain outside this release.
