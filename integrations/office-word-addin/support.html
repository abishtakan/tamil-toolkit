Support — Tamil Toolkit for Microsoft Word

Tamil Toolkit brings 100% client-side Tamil linguistic utilities to Microsoft Word (Windows, macOS, and Word on the Web).

Quick Start

  1. Open the தமிழ் (Tamil) tab in the Microsoft Word top ribbon.
  2. Use one-click ribbon buttons for Unicode Conversion, Repair, Akara Varisai Sort, Tamil Date, and Hyphenation.
  3. Click தமிழ் கருவிப் பலகை (Open Tamil Toolkit) to open the full multi-tool utility pane.

Hiding Soft-Hyphen Formatting Marks

If optional hyphens appear visible inside words on screen, press Ctrl + Shift + 8 (or toggle the ¶ icon on the Home tab) to hide non-printing formatting marks.
