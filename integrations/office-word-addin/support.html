Tamil Toolkit for Microsoft Word

Tamil Toolkit brings the fast, memory-safe Rust & WebAssembly Tamil linguistic engine directly into Microsoft Word (Windows, macOS, and Word on the Web). Document text is processed locally inside Word's browser runtime with zero document uploads.

Core Capabilities

  • Akara Varisai Sorting: Sort selected paragraphs, numbered lists, and table columns in authentic Mei-first (agara-v1) or school Uyirmei-first order.
  • Syllabic Hyphenation: Insert coda-binding soft hyphens (<w:softHyphen/>) or invisible wrap points without breaking pulli consonants (ஒற்று), preserving bold/italic OOXML formatting.
  • Unicode Cleanup & Tamil-Aware Find/Replace: Normalize NFC, collapse duplicate pulli marks, reorder visual kombu signs, and match across Mayangoli variants.
  • Live Typing, Transliteration & Legacy Conversion: Live Bamini typing, Tamil99 / Anjal Phonetic / ISO 15919 composer, and bidirectional conversion for Bamini, TSCII, TAB, TAM, Anjal, Vanavil, LT-TM, Senthamil, Shree-Tam, Softview, TBO, and Bharathi Braille (e.g. Bamini jkpo; → தமிழ்).
  • Numerals, Currencies & Tamil Date Stamps: Formal TN/SL number-to-words across 8 currencies (INR, LKR, SGD, MYR, USD, EUR, GBP, CAD), words-to-number (ITN), and Thirukanitham / Vakya Tamil date stamps.

Compatibility & Quick Start

Requires Microsoft Word supporting WordApi 1.3 or newer (Microsoft 365 on Windows, macOS, and Word on the Web).

  1. Install Tamil Toolkit from Microsoft AppSource or sideload the manifest.
  2. Open the தமிழ் (Tamil) tab in the Microsoft Word ribbon.
  3. Use one-click ribbon buttons for Unicode Conversion, Repair, Akara Varisai Sort, Tamil Date, and Hyphenation, or click தமிழ் கருவிப் பலகை to open the full 3-tab task pane.

Inserting & Removing Soft Hyphens

Use Hyphenate Selection or Hyphenate Body & Tables to add canonical syllabic soft hyphens, and Remove Hyphenation to strip only soft hyphens while preserving manual hyphens (-). If optional hyphens appear visible on screen, press Ctrl + Shift + 8 (or toggle the ¶ icon on the Home tab) to hide non-printing formatting marks.
