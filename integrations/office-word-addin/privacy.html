Privacy Policy — Tamil Toolkit for Microsoft Word

Effective Date: November 2026

Zero Data Collection Guarantee: All Tamil text conversion, normalization, sorting, numeral formatting, calendar calculations, syllabic hyphenation, and document inspection execute 100% locally on your device inside Microsoft Word using WebAssembly.

1. Document Content

Tamil Toolkit reads and modifies document text solely inside your active Microsoft Word session when you invoke a Ribbon/Taskpane command or explicitly enable Direct Live Typing mode (which monitors only the active paragraph locally until you stop it). No document text, metadata, or user content is ever transmitted to external servers, logged, or stored remotely.

2. Analytics & Tracking

This add-in uses no cookies, no third-party analytics, no advertising trackers, and no user account sign-in.

3. Open-Source Transparency

The complete source code is publicly auditable under the Apache-2.0 License at github.com/abishtakan/tamil-toolkit.
